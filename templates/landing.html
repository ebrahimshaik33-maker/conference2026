Built for Event Organizers
Who Demand the Best

From first registration to final keynote, The Conference Corner gives your team the tools to deliver a flawless attendee experience.

Multi-Day Programme Manager

Manage unlimited sessions, speakers, venues, and tracks across any number of conference days. Attendees navigate the schedule by day, filter by venue, track, or keyword, and bookmark sessions to build their personal agenda — all in real time.

15 Premium Themes

Switch between expertly crafted light and dark themes — from sleek corporate to vibrant academic — or build your own palette with custom colour and font pickers.

Live Push Notifications

Broadcast instant alerts to all subscribed attendees — session changes, delays, last-minute announcements — delivered to their browser even when the tab is closed.

One-Click CSV Import

Upload your spreadsheet programme and import in seconds. Smart auto-detect parsing supports comma, semicolon, tab-delimited and Moodle-style exports with clean error reporting.

Secure Admin Control Panel

A full admin dashboard with session editing, bulk status management, audit logging, database backup/export, and password-protected access — designed for event teams, not developers.

Google Analytics GA4

Track attendee engagement and session popularity with built-in GA4 support. Simply paste your Measurement ID in Settings — no code editing required.

Simple Setup

From Setup to Live in 3 Steps

No developers needed. No complex configuration. Just your programme data, your branding, and your audience.

1

Deploy & Configure

We set up your Conference Corner instance. You log in to the admin panel and configure your event name, dates, and choose from 15 beautiful themes in minutes.

2

Import Your Programme

Upload your conference schedule as a CSV spreadsheet. Our importer handles sessions, speakers, venues, tracks, abstracts, and external links automatically.

3

Go Live & Engage

Share the URL with your attendees. Send push notifications for real-time updates. Track engagement via Google Analytics. Your event, perfectly delivered.
